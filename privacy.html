Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Single Purpose & Data Collection

Horizons for Todoist provides year, month, and week planning inside Todoist. It processes the goals and preferences you enter. When you use the task picker, it reads relevant Todoist task details to let you find and link tasks; it does not edit or delete your regular tasks or projects.

The extension also temporarily uses your Todoist session for API requests. If you contact us, the information you submit and, unless you opt out, technical diagnostics may be processed to respond or troubleshoot.

2. Where Your Data is Stored

Your goals, settings, and preferences are stored in your browser using Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). If that API is unavailable, the extension can use page localStorage as a fallback. The Todoist session token is held temporarily in extension session storage.

Website theme and contact-form rate-limit preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. The extension also stores its installation date locally to calculate an approximate days-installed value for the uninstall feedback page; only the extension version and calculated day count are included in that page's URL.

Website analytics also uses localStorage for generated visitor and session identifiers. Website and extension analytics use the same PostHog project, with events labeled by surface; this does not automatically link your website visits to your extension identity.

3. Synchronization & Backups via Todoist

Sync and backups are separate features, both enabled by default. When enabled, Horizons sends the necessary data over HTTPS directly to Todoist's API using your active Todoist session.

  • Synced goals are stored in a marked task in a separate project named "Horizons sync (do not delete)".
  • Backup snapshots are stored as comments on a separate task in that project. Automatic backups keep the 14 newest snapshots; manual backups remain until deleted.

Horizons does not store your goals on a developer-operated server. JSON exports are saved wherever you choose on your device.

4. Analytics and Session Replay

This website uses PostHog's EU service to collect pageviews, traffic-source information, browser and device metadata, clicks and interactions, and session recordings. Recordings reproduce page structure, changes, scrolling, and interactions to help us understand use and improve the website. Install-button clicks and confirmed form submissions are tracked separately; an install-button click does not confirm an installation.

Website form input values are masked in recordings and excluded from automatic interaction capture. Names, email addresses, messages, and feedback text are not included in our custom analytics events. Network request and response bodies and headers, and console logs, are not recorded. Captured navigation URLs omit query strings and fragments; campaign attribution may be collected separately. Masking is not a guarantee that every possible piece of personal information on a page can be detected.

The extension also sends enabled usage analytics, error diagnostics, and session recordings to PostHog. Its analytics toggle controls extension collection, not website collection. Extension recordings cover Todoist and extension pages with selective masking of goal names, recognized task and project names, and passwords; other visible content can remain in recordings. We do not sell your data. See PostHog's privacy policy for information about the provider.

5. User-Initiated Support & Feedback

If you send us a message through the extension, we send your message, topic, and reply email (if provided) to Web3Forms so we can respond. Basic technical details are included by default to help us troubleshoot. You can turn this off before sending.

The website contact form sends your email, message, topic, and optional name to Web3Forms. After uninstalling, you can choose to send feedback through a survey. The survey includes your selected reason and any message or email you provide.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local extension data is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear its data in your browser. Uninstalling does not delete data already stored in Todoist.

  • You can delete individual backups in the extension's Backup Manager or remove the Horizons project from Todoist.
  • Turning sync or backups off stops new writes but does not delete existing Todoist data. Automatic backups keep the 14 newest snapshots; manual backups remain until deleted.
  • Web3Forms processes submitted form data under its own privacy practices and retention policies.
  • Analytics and recordings are retained according to the PostHog project's settings. Clearing browser storage or uninstalling the extension does not delete data already sent to PostHog. Contact us regarding deletion requests.

7. Compliance & Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the extension or its data practices change. Updates will be posted here with a revised date.

8. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the built-in Support & Feedback form in the extension popup or the contact form on our website.
