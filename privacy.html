Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Purpose and information we process

Horizons for Todoist provides year, month, week, and day planning inside Todoist. It processes goals, descriptions, preferences, and the Todoist task details needed to find and link tasks. It temporarily uses your Todoist session for approved API requests.

When the optional “Also create day tasks in Inbox” setting is enabled (on by default), Horizons creates and maintains matching Inbox tasks for day-lane tasks. In that mode the extension may create, update, complete, reopen, and delete those Inbox twin tasks, and may add a short marker comment so the twin can be recognized. Turning the setting off stops new twin writes; existing Inbox twin tasks remain until you delete them. Linking a Todoist task to a week, month, or year goal updates Horizons goal data and does not rewrite that task’s title or project.

When analytics is configured for a build, it is on. Analytics, error diagnostics, and session replay are sent to PostHog's EU service to understand use, investigate failures, and improve the extension. Analytics is separate from goal synchronization and backups. Turning sync, backups, or day-task twins off does not turn analytics off. There is no in-product analytics on/off switch.

If you submit a support, contact, or feedback form, the information you choose to submit is processed to respond or improve the product.

2. Local storage, synchronization, and backups

Goals, settings, preferences, and generated analytics identifiers are stored in your browser. Goal storage uses Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local), with page localStorage as a fallback when that API is unavailable. The Todoist session token is held temporarily in extension session storage for approved Todoist requests.

Sync and backups are separate features, both enabled by default. When enabled, they send goal data over HTTPS to your Todoist account:

  • Synced goals are stored on an extension-managed task in a separate project named "Horizons sync (do not delete)".
  • Backup snapshots are stored as comments on a separate task in that project. Automatic backups retain the 14 newest snapshots; manual backups remain until you delete them.
  • Day-lane Inbox twins (when that setting is enabled) are ordinary Todoist Inbox tasks in your account, separate from the sync project above.

Horizons does not operate a separate goal synchronization or backup server. Visible page content is also transmitted to PostHog when analytics is configured, as described below. Local/Todoist goal storage must not be interpreted as a promise that page content never leaves those locations. JSON exports are saved wherever you choose on your device.

The extension stores its installation date locally. The uninstall feedback page URL contains only the extension version and approximate days installed. Website theme and contact-form rate-limit preferences are stored locally. Website analytics also uses localStorage for generated visitor and session identifiers. Website and extension analytics can share a PostHog project with events labeled by surface; that does not automatically link website visits to your extension identity.

3. Analytics, errors, and session replay

This website uses PostHog's EU service to collect pageviews, traffic-source information, browser and device metadata, clicks and interactions, and session recordings. Recordings reproduce page structure, changes, scrolling, and interactions to help us understand use and improve the website. Install-button clicks and confirmed form submissions are tracked separately; an install-button click does not confirm an installation.

Website form input values are masked in recordings and excluded from automatic interaction capture. Names, email addresses, messages, and feedback text are not included in our custom website analytics events. Network request and response bodies and headers, and console logs, are not recorded. Captured navigation URLs omit query strings and fragments; campaign attribution may be collected separately. Masking is not a guarantee that every possible piece of personal information on a page can be detected.

When analytics is configured, the extension also sends usage analytics, error diagnostics, and session recordings to PostHog. There is no in-product analytics on/off switch. Extension recordings cover the whole Todoist page on every route, plus extension popup, support, and feedback pages, with selective masking of Horizons goal names, recognized Todoist task titles, task-label and project names, sidebar project/favorite names, and passwords. Task descriptions, goal descriptions, ordinary inputs, and personal or workspace names can remain visible. We do not sell your data. See PostHog's privacy policy.

4. Third-party services and sharing

  • Todoist processes enabled goal synchronization and backup data in your account. See Todoist's privacy policy.
  • PostHog processes analytics, error reports, and session recordings through its EU service when analytics is configured. See PostHog's privacy policy.
  • Web3Forms processes user-submitted support, contact, and feedback forms. See Web3Forms' privacy policy.

We do not sell your data or use it for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

5. User-initiated support and feedback

Extension support and feedback forms send your message, topic, and reply email (if provided) to Web3Forms. Basic technical diagnostics are included by default; you can turn them off before submitting. That choice applies only to the form submission, not to product analytics.

The website contact form sends your email, message, topic, and optional name. The optional uninstall survey sends your selected reason and any message or email you provide. Leaving a form without submitting does not submit a support request; when extension replay is active, ordinary form input values may still be present in recordings.

6. Retention, controls, and deletion

Local extension data is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear its data in your browser. Uninstalling does not delete data already stored in Todoist or PostHog.

  • You can delete individual backups in the extension's Backup Manager or remove the Horizons sync project from Todoist.
  • Turning sync or backups off stops new writes but does not delete existing Todoist data. Automatic backups keep the 14 newest snapshots; manual backups remain until deleted.
  • Turning “Also create day tasks in Inbox” off stops new Inbox twin writes but does not delete existing twin tasks; you can remove those tasks in Todoist like any other Inbox item.
  • PostHog session replay retention is 30 days. Other analytics and exception retention follow the PostHog project settings (confirmed). Clearing browser storage or uninstalling does not delete data already sent to PostHog.
  • Web3Forms processes submitted form data under its own privacy practices and retention policies.

For questions about access or deletion of information processed by the maintainers, use the contact routes below. Do not assume uninstalling or changing a setting automatically requests deletion from a third-party service.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the extension or its data practices change. Updates will be posted here with a revised date. Store declarations and this live policy must match the extension's actual behavior.

8. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the built-in Support & Feedback form in the extension popup or the contact form on our website.
