Your questions answered.

Where are my goals stored?

Goals and preferences are stored locally in your browser.

If you enable sync, Horizons also stores your goals in a separate project in your Todoist account.

Do I have to turn on sync?

No. Sync and backups are optional. You can keep your planning data on this device.

Can I back up or export my plan?

Yes. Horizons includes automatic snapshots and a JSON export so you can keep a copy of your plan.

Does Horizons create tasks in Todoist?

No. Horizons doesn't create or delete tasks in your regular Todoist projects or change your regular to-dos.

If sync or backups are enabled, Horizons creates a separate, hidden Todoist project to store its planning data.

What happens to my goals if I uninstall the extension?

If you uninstall Horizons without sync or backups enabled, your local data is removed.

If you have sync and/or backups enabled, your goals remain in a separate Todoist project and can be restored when you reinstall the extension. You can also restore a JSON backup.

Is Horizons free?

Yes. Horizons is free to use, with no separate sign-up or monthly fee.
